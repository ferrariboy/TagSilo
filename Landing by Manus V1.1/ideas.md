# TagSilo Standalone Marketing Site — Design Directions

## Three Initial Approaches

### Signal Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, editorial productivity system that turns the messy act of prospecting into a visible, ordered record. It should feel like a well-kept field ledger rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

**Probability:** 0.037

### Parchment Protocol

**Very Brief Intro:** A refined, print-inspired workspace that pairs warm paper, strong typographic rules, and quiet mechanical details. It makes lead organization feel deliberate and dependable.

**Probability:** 0.061

### Bright Workbench

**Very Brief Intro:** A high-clarity, industrial product surface with crisp blue, signal orange, and modular tools. The style emphasizes speed, utility, and the satisfaction of a clean handoff.

**Probability:** 0.082

## Chosen Direction: Signal Ledger

### Design Movement

**Editorial systems design** meets **modern field-notebook utility**. The page will avoid the familiar dark-neon “growth tool” look and instead make TagSilo feel like an orderly, intelligent layer for the work people already do on LinkedIn.

### Core Principles

1. **Literal before abstract.** Screens, labels, arrows, and copy should make the LinkedIn-to-Sheets workflow understandable at a glance.
2. **Calm operational confidence.** Spacious composition, measured typography, and reduced chrome should make a busy prospecting process feel more manageable.
3. **Proof through process.** The product flow—not performance claims, fabricated proof, or decorative technical language—does the selling.
4. **Human records, not faceless data.** Tags, notes, and group labels should visually foreground the relationship context that makes a saved lead useful.

### Color Philosophy

The primary surface is **paper-white with a barely warm cast**, signaling clarity without cold sterility. Deep forest-green ink represents thoughtful work and reliability, while a saturated **signal coral** marks calls to action and important handoffs. Soft mint is reserved for successful organization states. The intentional scarcity of bright color keeps attention on the action instead of filling the page with dashboard noise.

### Layout Paradigm

The site is organized as a **sequence of ledger entries**, not a stack of interchangeable centered SaaS bands. A narrow “field index” rail appears on large screens as a visual anchor, while main sections use left-anchored editorial headings, wide workflow panels, handwritten-margin-style annotations, and asymmetrical two-column moments. The central product story moves from profile to context to sheet in a clear left-to-right action path.

### Signature Elements

1. A **ledger-rule motif**: thin horizontal rules, small entry numbers, and annotation labels create a consistent record-keeping language.
2. A **coral transfer marker**: a small arrow-and-dot symbol identifies the moment a profile becomes an organized record.
3. A **cut-corner tag shape**: used in labels and visual chips to reinforce the brand’s tagging function without relying on a generic pill UI.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like turning a useful page in a well-designed workbook. Buttons provide a quick, tactile press; workflow cards reveal additional context on hover; FAQ answers open with calm, short motion. Interactions never obscure the primary path to installation.

### Animation

At first view, the hero’s profile, TagSilo context panel, and resulting sheet row enter in a short left-to-right sequence, reinforcing the product’s handoff. Horizontal rules subtly draw in rather than flash. Card and button motion remains under 220ms with a snappy ease-out. All non-essential movement is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

### Typography System

**Fraunces** supplies the editorial display voice for key statements and selected numerals, making the page memorable without feeling ornamental. **Manrope** handles body text, labels, navigation, and product UI because it remains highly legible at small sizes. Headlines should be confidently sized and left aligned; body copy stays short, active, and practical. Use uppercase tracking only for small ledger labels.

### Brand Essence

**TagSilo turns the LinkedIn profiles you discover into organized Google Sheet leads, without breaking your flow.**

**Personality:** Methodical, candid, reassuring.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are plainspoken, exact, and buyer-aware; CTAs name the next concrete action; microcopy reduces friction without hype. Avoid vague efficiency talk, hard guarantees, and cleverness that hides the product.

> “Save the lead while the context is still fresh.”

> “Add TagSilo to Chrome — Free.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a simple **open tag loop joined to a ledger line**: a rounded rectangular tag silhouette with one small circular hole and a clean line extending from its right edge. It is recognizable at favicon scale and functions as a standalone mark without typed letters.

### Signature Brand Color

**Transfer Coral — #E85D43.** It is reserved for the install CTA, key workflow arrows, and moments when a LinkedIn profile becomes a usable record.

## Style Decisions

The standalone site now uses **TagSilo Neon Lime — #A6FF26** as the signature brand color, as directed by the user. Dark ink surfaces establish contrast for the official repository lockup, while deep evergreen and subtle cyan-green gradients support the lime without competing with it. The official `tagsilo-neon-lime` logo lockup and icon replace the generated mark.

The official `brand` kit supersedes the initial Signal Ledger typography treatment. The standalone site now follows the documented **Cyber-Minimalist Precision SaaS** system: Plus Jakarta Sans for all headings and body copy, JetBrains Mono for tags and metrics, OLED Cyber Black and Obsidian surfaces, precision-aligned geometry, restrained glass overlays, and Electric Neon Lime as the only primary focal / transfer signal. Sky Cyan, Hot Pink, and Emerald remain controlled secondary system accents only.
