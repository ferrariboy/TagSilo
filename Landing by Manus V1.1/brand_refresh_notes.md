# Brand Refresh Notes

The official `tagsilo-neon-lime/logo-lockup.jpg` asset uses the neon-lime TagSilo mark and lime italic “Silo” wordmark on a near-black rectangular field. The standalone page should therefore move to a dark ink base, preserve the official lockup on dark surfaces, and use #A6FF26 as the primary interactive/accent color. The distinctive coral-led Signal Ledger treatment will be replaced with lime-forward controls and supporting green-cyan gradients.

## Official Brand Kit (source of truth)

The official brand kit specifies Electric Neon Lime `#A6FF26` as the primary focal point, CTA color, and high-contrast accent; Lime Hover is `#95EE18`. Its documented base palette is OLED Cyber Black `#06080D`, Obsidian Surface `#0B0F19`, Card `#0F172A`, white text `#F8FAFC`, and muted text `#94A3B8`, with Sky Cyan `#38BDF8`, Hot Pink `#FF0055`, and Emerald `#10B981` for controlled secondary accents. Typography is Plus Jakarta Sans for both headings and body, backed by Manrope, plus JetBrains Mono for metric and tag labels. The required visual identity is Cyber-Minimalist / Precision SaaS: clean precision-aligned geometry, a T-shaped tag silhouette with punch-hole and angled notch, high-contrast glow meshes, and glassmorphic card overlays.
