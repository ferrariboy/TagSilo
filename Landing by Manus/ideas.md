# TagSilo Landing Page — Visual Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied reference establishes the primary visual specification for this page: a high-contrast direct-response funnel with a dark, cinematic opening, an assertive red urgency strip, oversized editorial sans-serif headlines, tight modular content panels, and a long white storytelling body. The TagSilo version retains the reference’s structural rhythms—hero, pain framing, proof-style operational demonstrations, transformation, feature explanation, pricing, FAQ, and a closing conversion prompt—while replacing all product claims, labels, and visual assets with the TagSilo product narrative.

## Chosen Approach: The Systematic Professional

### Design Movement

Direct-response editorial meets systems-dashboard minimalism. The hero borrows the reference’s dramatic, high-contrast funnel language; the supporting sections shift into a crisp operational workbook aesthetic that makes TagSilo feel practical rather than promotional.

### Core Principles

1. **Action before abstraction:** Every major section makes the next workflow step visually legible.
2. **Controlled contrast:** Ink-black, off-white, graphite, and one electric lime create an immediate hierarchy.
3. **Editorial density:** Strong headings, conspicuous labels, and rule-based spacing create a purposeful funnel cadence.
4. **System evidence:** Product illustrations mimic the before-and-after movement from a LinkedIn profile to a clean lead pipeline.

### Color Philosophy

The black foundation signals focus and removes visual noise—the emotional opposite of a cluttered LinkedIn inbox. TagSilo lime is the owned action color: it marks capture, organization, and the moment a prospect becomes an actionable record. Warm white supporting sections create a clear shift from problem tension to operational clarity.

### Layout Paradigm

The page flows as a vertical argument rather than a conventional centered corporate website. Content moves through broad editorial fields, offset panels, narrow annotation bands, and deliberately uneven two-column stories. Generous full-width bands divide dark emotional states from light functional states.

### Signature Elements

1. Monospace indexed labels and small technical annotations.
2. Electric-lime rule accents, buttons, and status indicators.
3. Framed product-transfer diagrams that show profile details arriving in a structured capture card.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel decisive and useful. Calls to action give a small pressed response, profile panels lift on hover, and FAQs open cleanly without theatrical motion. The interface never obscures content or requires a user to decipher the next step.

### Animation

Above-the-fold content enters with short, staggered opacity-and-translate transitions. Graphic status dots pulse subtly, while horizontal tag rows move only on hover or during a reduced-motion-safe entrance. All interactions use a snappy custom ease-out and remain below 300ms.

### Typography System

**Manrope** serves as the dense, high-impact display face for headlines, navigation, and CTA labels. **IBM Plex Mono** serves labels, indices, tags, and technical metadata. Body copy uses Manrope at a calmer weight and line height. Typography scales from deliberate microcopy to oversized multi-line hero propositions.

### Brand Essence

**TagSilo is the zero-setup LinkedIn organization layer for sales professionals who want a clean, usable pipeline without leaving the profiles where work happens.**

Personality: **precise, resilient, unpretentious.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are candid and outcome-led; CTAs are concise, practical, and specific. Microcopy names the workflow friction rather than using generic software rhetoric.

Example lines:

> Stop letting warm leads disappear into tabs and threads.

> Capture the profile. Tag the intent. Sync the sheet.

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied TagSilo mark as the visual anchor: its simple lime “T” silhouette resembles a tag or a structured field marker. Pair it with the supplied TagSilo lockup wherever a full wordmark is appropriate; use the icon alone for system indicators and small interface marks.
