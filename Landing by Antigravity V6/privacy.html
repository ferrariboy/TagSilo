Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

TagSilo Pro ("TagSilo", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how our Chrome extension and web application handle user information.

1. Single Purpose & Data Minimization

TagSilo Pro exists solely to allow users to capture publicly visible contact data from LinkedIn profiles and sync that data directly into their own private Google Sheets spreadsheets.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Permissions

When you authenticate with Google OAuth 2.0, TagSilo requests limited scopes (spreadsheets, drive.file, userinfo.email) solely to create and append lead rows to your designated spreadsheet. We do not sell, transfer, or expose your Google data to third parties.

3. LinkedIn Data Extraction

TagSilo operates locally inside your browser on document_idle. It captures publicly visible profile fields (Name, Headline, Profile URL, Email, and Avatar) only when explicitly invoked by the user.

4. Third-Party Services & Payments

Payments for TagSilo Pro are securely processed by Creem. We do not store or process your credit card details on our servers.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, email us at support@tagsilo.com.
